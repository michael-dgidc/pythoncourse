Pizza ordering is the process of choosing a pizza, customizing it if needed, and submitting that choice to a pizza shop for preparation and delivery.

What it is:
- selecting size, toppings, crust, and quantity
- specifying customer details like name, address, and payment
- submitting an order so the restaurant can make the pizza

What it illustrates:
- a real-world example of a user request being turned into a structured task
- how input data can be collected, validated, and processed
- the flow from customer choice to order confirmation and fulfillment
